# Hyper authoring invariants

This contract governs instruction quality and suite behavior. `scripts/validate-hyper.mjs` owns deterministic inventory, metadata, reference and mirror checks; do not duplicate that inventory as an independent policy.

- Keep shipped skills portable and self-contained as a whole suite. Use job names for invocation and sibling-relative bundled references. Keep machine paths, maintainer tools, authorship and source history outside operational instructions; retain legal notices separately.
- Give each job one bounded result and an explicit session. Keep the router responsible for intent and continuation. Preserve model invocation for jobs and expose only the router in the menu.
- Create only artifacts that advance the request. Share path/authority/return ownership in the session contract, record shape in its template, ticket lifecycle in the graph reference and ticket shape in its template. A change to any contract includes its affected consumers.
- Preserve Matt's methods: tracer bullets and actual dependencies; tests at agreed behavior seams; deep interfaces and locality; independent Standards/Spec review; dedicated project standards; compact human evidence; concrete reversibility and blast radius; retrospectives tied to observed failures.
- Keep reviewer repair within an authorized build. Standalone review stays read-only. Carry exact authority through delegation; commits, pushes, publication and cleanup are separate gates. Serialize writes and retain unrelated work.
- Use imperative steps with observable completion. Keep each meaning with one owner, paragraphs on one source line and conditional detail behind direct links. Template shapes should not be copied into job prose.
- Distinguish passing mechanical checks from behavior evidence. Report sequential review, unavailable checks, unexecuted scenarios and other limits honestly.
